# Security

Found a vulnerability on the website or in one of our systems? Thank you, please tell us.

**How to report:** Privately via **Security → Report a vulnerability** in this repository, or through the request form on the website with the topic “Other”.

**What we ask:** Don’t access or change other people’s data, don’t disrupt the service, and give us time to fix it before you publish anything.

**What we do:** We confirm your report, keep you updated until the fix is live and name you as the finder if you want.
